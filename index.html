The complete program

Every moving part, connected.

01

Guided SOC 2 kickoff

Trust Services Category scoping, Type I vs. Type II selection with observation-period tracking, and a structured system-description builder.

02

Risk register (CC3)

Score likelihood × impact, assign owners, link risks to mitigating controls, and track remediation.

03

Continuous evidence automation

Deterministic collectors for GitHub, AWS, and Jira pull real evidence on a schedule; no AI guessing at facts an API can provide.

04

AI-assisted mapping, human-confirmed

AI proposes control mappings with cited reasoning; a human reviewer makes the final call, and both steps are logged.

05

Policy management

Upload, version, and track staff acknowledgment of required policies mapped to the controls they support.

A placeholder founding story

Compliance should feel like engineering work, not archaeology.

We watched engineering teams burn a quarter on SOC 2 evidence-wrangling and built the tool we wished existed.

That means treating scope as a decision, evidence as a traceable input, review as a human responsibility, and the final report as an output of the work—not the place where the work starts.
